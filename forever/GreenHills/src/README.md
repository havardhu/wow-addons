# Green Hills Pages (WoW: Forever)

Made for a bank alt collecting pages for The Green Hills of Stranglethorn. It shows which of the
15 needed pages you hold, which you still need, and which you've already sent to your main.
It doesn't look at the quest log.

## Use
- `/ghs` (or `/greenhills`) opens the list, grouped by chapter:
  - Chapter I: 1, 4, 6, 8
  - Chapter II: 10, 11, 14, 16
  - Chapter III: 18, 20, 21, 24
  - Chapter IV: 25, 26, 27
- Each page shows **Need**, **Have N** (with bags/bank split) or **Sent**.
- **Tick a page** once you've mailed it to your main. **Mark held as sent** ticks every needed
  page you hold, for right after you've mailed a batch. **Clear ticks** starts over.
- Pages the quest never asks for are listed at the bottom so you can sell them.
- Item tooltips (bags, auction house, chat links) say whether a page is needed, already sent,
  or not needed at all.

## Notes
- Open your bank once so the addon can count it. It remembers the bank between sessions.
- Ticks are account-wide, so every alt sees what's already been sent.

## Commands
`/ghs`, `/ghs list` (missing pages in chat), `/ghs clear`
